## State-of-the-art (EN)

Soil erosion by water is addressed by many funded projects, but few target the comparability of measurement methods. The Horizon Europe Mission Soil project EUROSION (101218843, 2025–2030) is building a multi-scale erosion monitoring network with a harmonised monitoring scheme and process-based models. Other EU-funded projects focus on modelling or land management, such as iMPACt-erosion (101062258), SED-RUNS (101033236) and SURE-SOIL (101236362), on sediment connectivity during extreme events, such as AsFoRESEEN (101109315), or on sediment source tracing, such as IMIXSED (644320). Earlier COST Actions coordinated neighbouring communities: ES1306 (2014–2018) on runoff and erosion connectivity and CA16219 (2017–2022) on harmonising UAS-based environmental monitoring. The published objectives of these projects do not include consensus-based protocols for experimental erosion measurement across scales and devices, including rainfall simulators, nor minimum reporting standards. This is the gap the Action addresses.

*(133 slov)*
